Petya's got <span class="tex-span"><i>n</i></span> non-transparent glasses, standing in a row. Note that the positions are indexed but the glasses are not.</p><p>First Petya puts a marble under the glass in position <span class="tex-span"><i>s</i></span>. Then he performs some (possibly zero) shuffling operations.
