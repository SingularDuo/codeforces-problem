Bob takes a sheet of paper and locates a Cartesian coordinate system on it as follows: point <span class="tex-span">(0, 0)</span> is located in the bottom-left corner, <span class="tex-span"><i>Ox</i></span> axis is directed right, <span class="tex-span"><i>Oy</i></span> axis is directed up. Pete gives Bob requests of three types: </p><ul> <li> <span class="tex-font-style-tt">add x y</span> — on the sheet of paper Bob marks a point with coordinates <span class="tex-span">(<i>x</i>, <i>y</i>)</span>.
